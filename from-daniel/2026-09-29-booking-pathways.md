## What it means (the lead's reading; confirm with Daniel where marked)
- **Booking is open to every non-emergency concern.** The only hard stop is an emergency, which is sent
  to 911 or the ER.
- **Each concern has one of two intake depths:**
  - **Quick-book** for straightforward concerns (tiles);
  - **Triage first** for complicated ones, done by the AI rather than a few static questions.
  Which concerns are "complicated" is **Daniel's call**. Examples given: haemorrhoids, diarrhoea and
  constipation.
- **Every pathway leads to the same queue:** tiles, the AI chat, the phone line, and messaging (a
  WhatsApp or text option, not yet decided).
- **No pathway is forced.** The AI is offered, not required, because some patients refuse to talk to
  an agent.
- **What we advertise shapes what arrives.** Easy concerns are what we advertise.
- **The product's job is to make the hard minority manageable** (triage, preparation, structure), so
  doctors stay happy.
