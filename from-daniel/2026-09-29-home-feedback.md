# Daniel's feedback on v2 Home — 29 Sep 2026

Source: WhatsApp from Dr. Daniel Pannozzo to Ani, with a marked-up screenshot of v2 Home
(`images/2026-09-29-home-markup.webp`). The quotes are verbatim.

## Call windows
Ani asked: "this is maximum window ranges right? can it not be 7 to 9". Daniel replied:
> "Yes - we have limited the number of windows. What that number is, i am not sure."

**Means:** the number of windows is deliberately limited. The exact number and times are still open
(OQ: window lengths and times).

## Home, from the markup
> "1. Can we place the timing info next to Good Evening or Good Morning or Good Afternoon whatever it
> might be"

> "2. I eliminated the extra stuff that isn't needed - crossed off"

> "3. I'd like the messenger function movable"

> "4. The AI function movable."

**What the markup shows** (read from the image; confirm with Daniel if unsure):
- **Timing.** An arrow runs from the greeting to the time pill in the top bar. He wants the local time
  and the window countdown beside the greeting, e.g. "Good evening, Dr. Pannozzo · 7:50 PM Toronto
  · 10m left". The screenshot is labelled "Can we make movable" near the top-right icons.
- **"Needs your attention," crossed off:**
  - the whole task row ("Rebook EDS referral · 4d overdue · Referrals · [MOA name]");
  - on the result row, everything after the value: the reference range, the diagnosis text, and the
    source and assignee.
- **"Needs your attention," kept:** the patient name and age/sex, the test, and the critical value with
  its arrow.
- **Movable panels.** The messenger (MOA chat) and the AI assistant should be panels the doctor can
  drag and place.

## Ani's idea (not yet decided)
> "I'm thinking to make chat one big menu on the left nav so it becomes more primary"

This means making chat a primary left-navigation destination rather than a floating panel. It is a
design option to weigh against Daniel's "movable" request.

## Decided 30 Sep (Ani)
- The time is in its own pill, before the theme button.
- The chat stays in the top bar, next to the profile, as a movable panel. The left-nav idea was tried
  and reverted.
