## 1. Who sends renewals, and what "picked up" means
> "Either can send - Japneet [has] prescribing experience, but I also have my favorite's
> pre-populated. So if I am f\*\*\*ing around, she sends it. If I think she'll f\*\*\* it up, then i send it."

**Means:**
- "Send it myself" and "Ask the MOA to send" are equal choices. It's the doctor's call each time, so
  neither is the default.
- The doctor keeps **favourite prescriptions pre-populated**: saved scripts he reuses.

## 2. Reviewing versus signing off
> "Reviewing is separate, all it means is that the Doctor is assessing - it means no further action is
> necessarily to be taken until such time as I review and sign off. To sign off is typically an action.
> So I 'sign off' on the chart - what does that mean? It means the chart is completed. What does that
> mean? It means that i've finalized the visit."
>
> "To Fax is to 'sign off' on the script"
> "To submit a bill is to 'sign off' on your billing."
> "It is action oriented."
> "The Doctor has approved this, meaning my a\*\* is on the line."

**Means:**
- *Reviewed* is a state: the doctor is assessing, and nothing happens yet.
- *Sign off* is the doctor's accountable approval, and it is always an action:
  - signing off the chart = finalizing the visit;
  - faxing a script = signing off the prescription;
  - submitting a bill = signing off the billing.
- The product should use the language of sign-off for these approving actions, and make clear that
  each one carries the doctor's name.

## 3. "Since last visit"
> "Yes ....exactly - I need a 'Care Plan' and even the last note summary is good too"

**Means:** both are wanted at the top of the chart: the **care plan** (the narrative of what we're
doing) and a **summary of the last note**.

## 4. Where weights come from
> "The weights come from me asking the patient. I get my patient's to work. It means I get them to do
> their blood pressures, their weights etc."

**Means:** measurements are **reported by the patient**. The patient enters their BP and weight
(portal or on the call), and the doctor sees the trend.

## 5. Raw lab PDFs
> "yes....it is madness that I am opening up raw pdf's to find out what the f\*\*\* is going on."

**Means:** results must be data (values and trends by test), not files.

## 6. Blood work uploaded by the patient
> "The fact that i need to do this is wild"
> "We need API access so that we get the results from the source. I am relying on patients to help me."

**Means:**
- The real fix is getting results directly from the lab or source through integration (an API).
- A patient upload is only a fallback, and it should be marked as patient-supplied.
