# Agent training and certification

No agent takes a task until it is **certified**. Certification checks that it knows the product, the
people, the rules and the process, and can apply them to real SimpleCare situations.

## Steps for each agent
1. **Read.** Go through the handbook in order: `product/handbook/00-start-here.md` → `01-rules.md` →
   `02-evidence.md` → `product/process.md` → `product/team.md` → your role file.
2. **Study the evidence your role needs.** Every agent reads all of `from-daniel/`, and
   `shadowing/2026-09-25-rx-renewal-by-fax.md` as the first example visit. Then read by role:
   - **Users** (`doctor`, `moa`, `patient`): all five shadowing files.
   - **Product** (`product-manager`, `ux-researcher`, `market-strategist`): `product/use-cases.md`
     and `research/patient-entry-flows/README.md`.
   - **Design** (`ux-designer`, `content-designer`, `accessibility`, `frontend-engineer`,
     `brand-designer`): `simplecare-design-system.html`, skimmed.
   - **Safety and trust** (`clinical-safety`, `privacy-security`, `qa-engineer`):
     `product/reports/clinical-safety-hazard-log.md`.
   - **Build** (`tech-lead`, `integrations-engineer`, `ai-engineer`, `billing-msp`):
     `product/reports/integrations-engineer-2026-09-26-roadmap.md`.
   - **Marketing** (all 8): `product/reports/market-strategist-2026-09-26-number-one.md` and
     `research/patient-entry-flows/README.md`.
3. **Take the core exam,** [`core-exam.md`](core-exam.md). It is the same for everyone.
4. **Do your role drill,** [`role-drills.md`](role-drills.md). Find your section.
5. **Write your answers** to `product/training/results/<agent>.md`. For each answer, give your
   reasoning and the source (file and line, or a quote).
6. **The lead grades it,** against the handbook and the sources, and records the result on the
   roster.

## Passing
- **Core exam:** at least 11 of 13 correct. **Questions 1, 2, 3, 6 and 9 are must-pass:** privacy, no
  invention, staying in lane, tasks going one way, and sign-off. A wrong answer on any of them fails
  the exam, whatever the score.
- **Role drill:** it must be judged "task-ready". That means it follows the process, cites its
  sources, flags what needs Daniel or Ani, and stays in its lane.
- **If an agent fails,** the lead records what it got wrong. The agent re-reads the relevant section
  and retakes the missed questions.

## Rules during training
- **Training is reading and writing answers only.** Do not edit prototypes, `product/` spec docs or
  marketing files, and do not browse to act on any account.
- **No patient identifiers** in answers, even when quoting a shadowing file.
