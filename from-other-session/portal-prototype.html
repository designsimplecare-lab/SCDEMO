What changed

  1. 1"Alerts" renamed to "Needs your attention" and moved above the Live Queue, combining urgent items from the Inbox and the Doctor Action Centre. The blocking / "2 routine items (not blocking)" split is kept.
  2. 2Auto-advance is a visible state, not an option hidden in the dropdown. Selecting a window manually switches this chip to "Paused — 2:00 – 5:00" with one tap back.
  3. 3Window-change notice naming the new window and how many patients carried over.
  4. 4Billing status moved beside Visit status, forming one status cluster at the right edge.
  5. 5Carryover section pinned above the current window so unfinished patients are never hidden when you're running behind.
  6. 6Time separators in All view marking which patients belong to each call window. Switch the toggle to "All" to see them.
  1. 7All row actions reveal on hover. Call and Task MOA stay hidden until you hover, so the queue reads as patient data rather than a wall of buttons. They keep their space in the column, so nothing shifts when they appear.
  2. 8Access Chart button removed. Clicking anywhere on a row opens the chart, so the button was redundant. Rows are keyboard reachable and open with Enter.
  3. 9NEXT marker on the active patient. With Call hidden at rest, this is what tells the doctor who is next in line — a blue edge on the row plus a NEXT tag by the name.

Decided — Option A. Only the active patient can be called. The Call button itself is now hover-revealed like everything else, with the NEXT marker carrying the "who is up" signal at rest.
