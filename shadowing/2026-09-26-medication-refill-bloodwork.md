# Shadowing 5 — new patient after a TIA, no refill until the bloodwork (current production)

Source: a screen recording and its Loom transcript, supplied by Ani, 26 Sep 2026, titled "Medication
Refill and Blood Work Follow-Up", 5 min 0 s long. This is the current production SimpleCare (chart
modal over the Daysheet). Patient details are left out. Clinical history is given in general terms only.

Frames were taken every 5 s. The screen hardly changes: after the call connects, the only movements are
one small scroll of the chart modal and the pointer resting on buttons. Loom timestamps match video
time. The transcript's first block (0:02) runs across the dialling, so the greeting itself falls at
about 22 s, when the call connects. The call timer is quoted where it helps.

## Patterns across the five visits
- **The note is not written during the call, 5 out of 5.** Here the caret sat in an empty note for over
  four minutes while the patient gave a full history: a hospital stay, a new diagnosis, three new
  medications with dates, and a planned test. This was the richest history of the five, and none of it
  was captured.
- **Continuity lives outside the chart, 2 out of 5.** The same question about other platforms was asked
  in shadowing 1 and here, in both cases in front of a chart that said "no previous notes". The chart
  cannot answer it.
- **What he needs comes from outside, now 4 out of 5.** Shadowing 1: other platforms. Shadowing 3:
  outside operative reports, asked of the patient by email. Shadowing 4: outside lab PDFs. Here: a
  hospital admission and its medications, and an upcoming result from bloodwork ordered elsewhere,
  which the patient is asked to upload. Shadowing 3 and this visit share a pattern: **the doctor asks the
  patient to send something, and nothing tracks it.**
- **Calling, wrong 3 out of 5.** "Call Again" resets instead of dialling, here and in shadowing 3.
  Shadowing 1 had the "fifty presses". Shadowing 2 and 4 were clean.
- **The action row goes unused, 5 out of 5.** Here the visit's outputs were a decision not to
  prescribe, a follow-up pending a result, and education to send. None of these is a button in the row.
- **The visit's real outputs are not orders.** "No refill needed until …", "review the bloodwork
  together", "send home BP instructions". The product has a place for prescriptions and requisitions,
  but none for these.
- **A new patient who wants a family doctor.** Shadowing 1 was a one-off renewal. This patient lost
  their family doctor and wants ongoing care, and the doctor pitched continuity out loud. Nothing on the
  chart marks the patient as wanting to stay with him.

## What v2 already covers
- **Calling.** One press dials whatever the label says, and a dropped call shows "Call dropped at … ·
  Redial" with a timer that counts the whole contact (UC-03). This covers the two-press fault seen here.
- **An honestly empty note.** v2's placeholder is "Document the visit...". The ear-pain example and the
  ticking "Note documented" stamp are production problems only.
- **Finalize.** "Finalize today's visit" asks first when the note is empty or still holds template text,
  and a signed visit locks the renewal card. Not reached in this recording.
- **The renewal card** (dose, quantity worked out from directions × days, a check step, fax status, "Ask
  MOA to send"). Not needed today, because no refill was given. Its medication lines come from the
  chart's medication list, so for a new patient with nothing on file the card would open empty.
- **Since last visit.** Correctly hidden when there is no previous visit, which is the case here. It
  lists pending items from SimpleCare's own open orders and unread inbox results only, so bloodwork
  ordered elsewhere would not appear in it.
- **Critical result on the chart.** Not relevant here.
- **Patient instructions exist as a care-plan item.** "Send patient instructions" releases them to the
  patient portal, and the scribe can draft a "Patient instructions" line. The hypertension care item
  already flags "Valid home BP series needed" under Hypertension Canada. What is missing is the content
  itself (a standard home-BP handout) and a way for the patient to return readings.
- **Patient upload, partly.** The patient portal has a general "Add a document" ("Lab result from
  elsewhere…"), saved to health records. It is not linked to a request from the doctor, and the physician
  portal has nothing that shows the upload arrived.
- **Not built:** the other-platforms question at intake (UC-05, flagged after shadowing 1).

## What it changes in v2
- **A decision not to prescribe is a first-class outcome.** The renewal card gets "No renewal today",
  with a reason ("supply lasts until mid-Oct; awaiting bloodwork") that goes into the note as one line
  and onto the medication as "next review after bloodwork". The supply date is kept, so if the result is
  late the chart shows "supply runs out in 5 days".
- **Medications started elsewhere go on the list during the call.** For a patient with no medications on
  file, the medications section offers "Add what the patient takes": drug, dose, "started in hospital
  (month)", "dose increased (month)", and source "patient-reported". Three lines here. This fills the
  empty renewal card for the next visit, and it is the history the doctor otherwise has to ask for
  again.
- **History in one line per event.** A "New to SimpleCare" chart shows a short intake block the doctor
  can fill from the call: previous family doctor (stopped practising, no transfer of records yet), recent
  hospital stay (reason, length, month), new diagnoses. This goes to the problem list: TIA, with the three
  medications linked to it as secondary prevention.
- **Expected outside results.** "Bloodwork booked for early October, ordered elsewhere" can be added as
  a pending item without a SimpleCare requisition. It shows in "Since last visit" at the next visit and
  under the care plan now, as "Waiting on the patient: upload lipid and liver results after the draw".
  When the date passes with nothing uploaded, it turns overdue for the doctor, who decides whether to
  task the MOA. Nothing becomes a task on its own.
- **A request to upload goes to a named place.** The doctor's words were "attach it to the chart under
  the follow-up section". The patient portal gets a matching item: "Dr. <name> asked for your bloodwork
  results · Upload", tied to the pending item above. An upload there lands on the physician's Home and in
  the chart's Results with "sent by the patient", and it closes the pending item. The general "Add a
  document" stays for anything else.
- **Standard patient education, sent with one press.** Patient instructions get a small library of
  standard handouts, starting with home blood-pressure measurement (the Hypertension Canada method:
  morning and evening readings in duplicate over 7 days, rested and seated). The doctor picks one during
  or after the call, it is released to the patient portal, and the note records "Home BP instructions
  sent". This is the explicit example the doctor promised here.
- **Home readings come back as data.** The same handout carries a readings form in the patient portal.
  The series feeds the chart's "Last blood pressure" and the hypertension care item's "Valid home BP
  series needed" gap, so the review after the bloodwork has both results and readings.
- **Mark the patient as staying for continuity.** When a new patient wants ongoing care, the doctor
  can mark them "Continuing with me" (attachment to his panel). The chart header shows it, and the
  follow-up after the bloodwork is booked with the same doctor. This is the physician-side end of the
  patient's "Family Practice" choice at booking (UC-24).
- **Ask about other platforms at intake, now seen twice.** "Have you been seen on another virtual
  platform (e.g. Tia Health, Rocket Doctor)?" becomes an intake question. The answer shows beside "No
  previous notes", so the chart can answer what the doctor asked in shadowing 1 and 5.
