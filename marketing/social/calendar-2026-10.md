## Rules for every post

- **Facts:** every claim is quoted from simplecare.ca or simplecare.ca/faq, checked 26 Sep 2026.
  There are no invented numbers, reviews or testimonials.
- **Wait times:** no wait-time estimates. Daniel wants the queue number, not minutes.
- **No drugs, conditions or advice:** no prescription-drug names. No weight-loss, ED or hair-loss
  topics, even though the site lists them. No individual medical advice.
- **No patients:** no patient faces, stories or stock photos posed as patients. Visuals are UI
  screens (demo data only), typography, or team members who have given written consent.
- **Calls go outward:** the doctor calls the patient. Patients call the office line,
  (778) 949-APPT, only for bookings, changes and support.
- **Links:**
  - Facebook posts link to `https://simplecare.ca/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10&utm_content=<post id>`.
  - Instagram says "Link in bio"; the bio link is `utm_source=instagram`.
  - UTMs carry the post ID only, never a condition or concern. `performance-marketing` confirms the
    tracking plan.
- **Visuals (for `brand-designer`):**
  - SimpleCare Paper background `#EEF0F4`, white cards with a 20px radius, Manrope, ink text
    `#17181C`, and `#4353E8` for the CTA only.
  - Navy `#162660` is for the logo only. No lime, no script logo.
  - 1080×1350 for feed images, 1080×1920 for Reels and Stories.
  - Body text no smaller than 40px at 1080 width.
- **Accessibility:** alt text on every image. Reels have burned-in captions plus the auto-caption
  track. Hashtags go at the end in CamelCase, and captions avoid emoji strings.
