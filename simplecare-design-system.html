SimpleCare Paper

The SimpleCare design system, drawn from the light dashboards of RonDesignLab and rebuilt for a clinic: ink on a cool paper page, depth from lightness alone, colour spent only on real exceptions, and nothing a physician reads under 14px.

Principles

  1. Ink on paper: one flat, cool page and white panels. Depth comes from lightness steps (1.08 to 1.17:1), never from a shadow, a border or a gradient.
  2. Colour is a signal, not a style. Words are ink. Red, amber and green appear only on the glyph of a real exception, and blue only where SimpleCare points: the Assistant, the 'now' mark, unread, AI and focus.
  3. A stroke means you can press or choose it. Only the secondary button, the billing chip and the selected answer carry one. Cards, bands, badges and status words never do.
  4. One black per zone. Black shows where you are (the nav) and what to do next (the primary). Every other selection is a white segment on a grey track.
  5. Numbers speak quietly. Time and counts are big light numerals. Values in rows are firm 600 ink. Units always follow on the baseline, in grey.
  6. Space before lines. Group with 16 to 32px, separate rows with 4px gaps and a hover fill, and use a hairline only as the rare split inside a card.
  7. Built for all-day reading. Nothing a physician reads is below 14px, nothing is uppercase, and every control is at least 44px.

Components

Page and foundations

Restyles: body, .app, .topbar, sidebar nav circles, page titles (Inbox / Behdis Maleki), Mage icon sizing, focus ring, tone utilities

A single flat --paper page, with no gradient and no watermark (soma-5 Navexa #EFF3F4; lendora-3 #EFF3F6). The chrome floats: the topbar is paper at 96% with no bar or rule, like the title and back button that sit directly on the page in lendora-3. The page title is 28/500, not 700. The reference sets titles Regular (lendora-3 Property Map, soma-5 Warehouse 1). Icons default to 1.1em of the adjacent text (constraint 4). Tone classes colour an icon only, never a word. Sidebar nav items are 44px circles, transparent at rest, --panel on hover and --ink when current, so only one black circle is on screen instead of five white ones.

Inbox

Card surfaces

Restyles: .box, .stat, .day-bar, inbox card, review cards, source-document frame, the rare in-card divider

A card is a --panel fill with a 20px radius, no border and no shadow. The reference shows no measurable drop shadow (lendora-3: luminance under the white pills never drops below the page). Cards sit 16px apart with the paper showing between them, like the 2-3px seams in quickbooks-4 that we widen for calm. Inside a card, group with space first. A group that needs a container is a --wash block at 12px (an inset). The only line is 1px --line, inset to the content column (quickbooks-4: #F6F6F6 divider stopping short of the card edges). The source-document frame is the one bordered surface, because it depicts paper. Padding is 24px for lists and 28px for review; list cards use 8px because their rows carry their own padding.

Primary button

Restyles: Accept & assign, Sign off, Start call, and any single 'do this' action in a zone

A 44px ink pill with an 18px Mage icon and a 15/600 label, with 16px padding on the icon side and 20px on the label side. It is the darkest object in its zone (lendora-4: black 'Search' pill; soma-4: black NFC pill; salesforce-3: black active pill). Allow one per card. In dark mode it becomes a light fill with a dark label through --ink and --on-ink. It is never accent-filled, because the accent fill belongs to the Assistant alone.

Secondary and quiet buttons

Restyles: Modify, Back to inbox, Hide source document, toolbar utilities

Secondary buttons are outlined, per the hard constraint. The shape is a 44px pill, transparent, with a 1px --edge stroke drawn as an inset box-shadow so hover never shifts the layout, a black 18px icon and a 15/600 ink label. --edge is 4.39:1 on panel. It is lighter than today's ink stroke (review-high-1440), but unlike the reference's 1.1-1.2:1 hairlines (salesforce-3 pills, lendora-3 circles) it still reads as pressable. On hover the stroke goes to ink and the fill to --wash. Quiet buttons are the same pill with a --control fill and no stroke, for in-card utilities (lendora-4 'Apply for Loan' and 'Save Property' grey pills).

Icon button

Restyles: Topbar play / moon / chat / bell, card-header actions, sidebar collapse arrow

A 44px circle with a 20px ink Mage icon and no stroke. Each button is one step off its surface: --control on a panel, --panel on the paper (the topbar). This follows lendora-4, where white circles sit on the grey page, and soma-5, where Navexa uses grey-filled buttons inside cards. Unread is an 8px accent dot at the top right, ringed 2px in the button's own fill so it cuts cleanly (soma-5: 4-slice-px dot on the bell). It turns --crit only while a critical item is waiting. The count moves into the opened panel, so the red '3' badge goes away. Every icon button needs an aria-label and a tooltip.

Rules it keeps, and what it left out

How the hard rules are honoured

  • 1. All text is ink; colour only on icons, strokes and dots: every text token is --ink, --ink-soft or --ink-faint. --crit-text is used only by .ds-an__val--crit, for a genuinely critical value. Status and accent colours are applied through .ds-tone-*, .ds-trend--*, dots and strokes, never through a text colour. Words on fills are --on-ink (primary, set filter, current nav) or --on-accent (the Assistant), which are the fill's own ink, not a hue.
  • 2. Tinted surfaces are grey, no red fills: the critical band is --control (#EBEDF1 / #2C3036). The AI summary and recall banner are --wash. The crit, warn and good washes are retired as surfaces. 'Claim rejected' is a --panel pill with a --crit stroke. The only coloured fill in the system is the Assistant's flat accent.
  • 3. Buttons are 44px, every button has an icon, secondary buttons are outlined, icons are black: .ds-btn-primary, .ds-btn-secondary, .ds-btn-quiet, .ds-bill, .ds-filter, .ds-seg__item, .ds-icon-btn, .ds-assist, .ds-status-menu and .ds-toast__action all use --h-control (44px), and choice options are 48px. Every action example carries a Mage icon, drawn in ink or --on-ink. Secondary is an outline in 1px --edge (4.39:1), darkening to ink on hover. Interpretation: segments and filter triggers are selectors, not actions. Their glyph is the now-dot or the caret, so we do not add five decorative clock icons to the call-window bar. If Ani wants the rule literal, add a 16px Mage clock to each segment.
  • 4. Mage icons only, about 1.1x the text: every icon is a <span class="ic" data-icon> using names verified on Iconify's mage set (exclamation-circle-fill, exclamation-triangle, arrow-up, stars-c, search, caret-down-fill, multiply, check, check-circle-fill, chevron-right, file-2, flag, user-check, edit, eye-off, notification-bell, message-dots, clock). The default is 1.1em. Component sizes follow the ratio: 18px beside 15-16px, 16px beside 14px, and 20px in 44px icon buttons.
  • 5. No capital-letter styling: there is no text-transform anywhere. Headings, table headers, group labels and chips are sentence case. The uppercase labels in soma-3 and soma-4 were rejected.
  • 6. Few strokes: cards, bands, the AI summary, chips and status words carry no stroke. Strokes remain only on the secondary button, the billing chip (1.5px ink, the one stroked control in a queue row), the selected decision option, and the source-document frame, which depicts paper. Rows are separated by 4px gaps, not rules.
  • 7. Exception-based display: resolved billing states become plain text, 'Verified' renders nothing, routine rows get no icon or band, the green presence dot is removed, and the red count badge becomes an unread dot. A routine-only screen renders in ink, greys and one blue pill.
  • 8. Nothing under 14px: the type scale floor is 14px (units, meta, helpers, table headers, chips). Values are 16-20px and stats 32-40px. The 12px build stamp is the only exception and is not physician-read. Light 300 is used only at 32px and above; values in rows are 600.
  • 9. Dark mode: every colour, shadow and weight token has a :root[data-theme="dark"] value. Components reference tokens only, so primary and nav flip to a light fill with a dark label, raised segments stay lighter than their track (--raised #40454D), dot rings match their button fill, and --shadow-float becomes a 6% rim. The dark critical value is #FF7A70 (5.2:1 on the band).
  • Dr. Pannozzo, 'balloon party' and red for critical only: red appears only when a critical item is on screen (its icon, value, arrow and the optional bell dot), and amber only for high. Blue has five narrow jobs and never touches text or priority. Three bands map to three treatments: critical has band plus red, high has an amber glyph, routine is ink.
  • Dr. Pannozzo, call window and clock at a glance: 40px light numerals for local time and time left, the tick ruler with an accent now-dot, and the segmented window bar with a live dot, grouped in one Home card.

  • Frosted glass, backdrop blur, soft rims and inner glows (soma-5 Navexa Report panel, soma-4 glucose card, the quarn and lendora glass cards).
  • Every gradient: Salesforce's purple-pink-orange-teal tiles (salesforce-3), Lendora's yellow-to-periwinkle price panel (lendora-3/4), Quarn's neon-green glow (quarn-2), Raktor's orange danger glow (raktor-1), and our own --gradient-agent and body gradient.
  • Neon lime and yellow accents (#F0F423 in quickbooks-3/4, #F0FE71 in soma, #FED96A in lendora-3). Next to our amber 'high' they read as caution. Blue #4353E8 stays our only accent.
  • Large accent-filled or tinted regions: QuickBooks' lime left third and lime active tab (quickbooks-4), the Soma lime sheet (#F8F6E2), and Lendora's periwinkle tiles (#F0F3FD). Our tinted surfaces are grey.
  • Saturated status fills with white text: the Navexa 'Done' #0AAA2F and 'Error' #FD3B3C chips (soma-5) and Salesforce's pink 'Target' pill (salesforce-3).
  • Red notification dots for ordinary counts (the Navexa and Salesforce bells). Red means critical only.
  • Hairline strokes of 1.1-1.2:1 on pressable controls (the salesforce-3 outline pills, the lendora-3 up-right circles). Our press stroke is at least 3:1 (--edge 4.39:1).
  • Pale-grey microcopy near 2:1 and 9-12px labels (lendora-3 'Budget $1,850', and the Navexa and Salesforce micro-labels). Comparators use --ink-faint at 14px or larger.
  • Superscript and prefix units ($ superscripts in salesforce-3; the '$3,420' and '%8.4' prefixes in lendora-3), which are a misread risk in a clinical record. Units trail on the baseline.
  • Coloured digits and delta text, including QuickBooks' lime '-1.3%' tags. Direction lives on the Mage arrow, and only a critical value may be red ink.
  • Uppercase labels ('SOMA 3 SENSOR', 'GLUCOSE' in soma-3/4).
  • Light 300 numerals inside rows and cells, and Salesforce's extended face and Quarn's condensed face for data. Raktor's monospace UI chips are rejected too: mono is for the as-received document only.
  • QuickBooks' 0-radius square controls and dot-grid texture, and Soma's dot-grid background.
  • Pill chip labels above every numeral (the 'Raised' and 'Remaining' chips in salesforce-3), which decorate the normal case.
  • Literal dotted-text leaders (soma-0 and soma-4 'Signal ····· Strong'). Key-value lists use a fixed label column and space instead.
  • Decorative art: wireframe funnels and globes (quickbooks-4), 3D buildings, sensors and drones, map backdrops, photo-avatar stacks, and all studio and client logos. Kora is excluded entirely.
  • Overruled from the studies: keeping the warm #F1F1EF paper and a warm #EFEFEC control fill. We chose the cool #EEF0F4 ladder, in the family of the 234° accent and matching the soma-5 and lendora-3 pages.
  • Overruled from the studies: a black selected segment on the call-window bar and an ink-filled Assistant. Both would compete with the primary button and the nav for the zone's one black. The bar uses a white segment on a grey track, and the Assistant is flat accent.
  • Overruled from the studies: 700-weight values and titles, and 36/500 stat numerals. The system stops at 600 and uses 300 only at 32px and above, which matches the reference's Regular titles and Light numerals.
  • Overruled from the studies: secondary buttons stroked in --line-strong (1.5:1, too faint to read as a button) and in full ink (heavier than the calm target). --edge sits between them at 4.39:1.
  • Overruled from the studies: a 12px-radius filter trigger and 10/14/22px radii. There are only three radii (20, 12, pill) plus the circle.
  • Overruled from the studies: a 1px --line stroke on textareas. Fields are a --control fill, and the focus ring draws the edge.
