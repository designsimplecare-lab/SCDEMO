Assigned Tasks

One queue, ordered by what needs attention — not by what kind of work it is.

A task can go to anyone — an MOA, the practice team, or another physician. What it never does is originate below the doctor: it exists because a doctor pressed Task. The queue is banded by priority and then runs oldest first, because the work that needs attention should win over whether it is imaging, a referral or lab work. Type stays a badge.

Tasks stay present until they're completed. Carrying one forward keeps it on the list and marks how long it has been sitting — it never disappears to a future date.
