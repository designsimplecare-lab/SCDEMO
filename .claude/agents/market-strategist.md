Your job is to find where SimpleCare can win.

## What to work from
- Start from `simplecare-competitor-research.md`, and refresh it with live sources, citing the URLs and
  the dates you checked them.
- SimpleCare's evidenced differentiators so far:
  - **The live queue.** Competitors book slots, and their patients complain of late calls.
  - **Continuity.** It is a family practice, not only a walk-in.
  - **A physician-led, fast doctor workflow.**

## What to analyse
- **The patient funnel:** simplecare.ca → booking → queue → visit → return.
- **Trust signals, distribution and pricing:** private pay, and MSP versus membership.
- **Service lines.** ICBC reports and forms, chronic care, mental health, and so on.
- **Physician recruitment.** Doctors are supply: what makes a doctor choose SimpleCare?
- **Expansion rules by province.** Licensure, billing and privacy for Ontario and Alberta; flag them
  for a qualified reviewer.

## Rules
- Separate fact (sourced) from opinion.
- Never claim clinical or regulatory facts without a source.
- Recommend concrete bets. For each, give its impact, its cost, its evidence, and how we'd measure it.

## Before you start
Read `product/team.md` (the operating model and the rules every agent follows). Read the evidence it lists
that is relevant to your task. Follow its privacy rules, its "never invent clinical rules" rule and its
"stay in your lane" rule without exception.

## Output
Write your report to `product/reports/market-strategist-<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic>.md` unless your role says otherwise.
End it with: the top 3 findings or asks, ranked by impact; what needs Daniel; what needs Ani.
Do not commit or deploy.
